Privacy Notice

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Pripar AI is a brand of IJ Semiconic Private Limited. This notice explains what personal information we collect through pripar.com and how we use it. Contact: hello@pripar.com.

What we collect

  • Contact form: the name, email address and message you send us, delivered through our form provider, Formspree.
  • Payments: when you pay, you enter your details on Razorpay's page. We receive your name, email, phone number, amount and payment status. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your country: when you first visit, the site asks a lookup service (api.country.is, or ipwho.is as a back-up) which country your connection is in, so we can show prices in your currency. Your internet address is sent to that service for this purpose and we do not store it.
  • Your region choice: saved in your browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, provide and invoice the services you buy, show the right prices, keep our records, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Who else handles it

  • Razorpay (payments)
  • Formspree (contact form)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting)
  • api.country.is and ipwho.is (country lookup)

Some of these providers are based outside your country, so your information may be processed in other countries, including India and the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and project records for as long as we need them to deal with your project and our legal and tax duties, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how we use it. This includes rights under the UK and EU GDPR, the Australian Privacy Act, Singapore's PDPA, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 and US state privacy laws such as the CCPA. Email hello@pripar.com and we will respond promptly.

Changes

We may update this notice and will change the date at the top when we do.
